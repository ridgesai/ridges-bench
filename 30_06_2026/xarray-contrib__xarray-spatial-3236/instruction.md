Fix `xrspatial.accessor.XrsSpatialDataArrayAccessor.open_geotiff` on `xrspatial.accessor.XrsSpatialDataArrayAccessor` so that GeoTIFFs opened through a DataArray's `.xrs.open_geotiff(...)` preserve caller-intended dask chunking when reprojection occurs via `coregister=True` or `auto_reproject=True`. From a dask-backed caller with explicit `y` and `x` chunks, the reprojected/coregistered result must remain dask-backed and use `(caller_first_y_chunk, caller_first_x_chunk)` rather than the reprojection default `(512, 512)`. When an explicit `chunks=` argument is provided with either `coregister=True` or `auto_reproject=True`, the reprojected output must use that explicit chunking even if the caller is not dask-backed, such as `(2, 2)` for `chunks=2`.
