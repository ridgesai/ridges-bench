Bounded great-circle proximity operations currently diverge for dask-backed rasters when longitude wraps at the antimeridian. Update the public functions `xrspatial.proximity.proximity`, `xrspatial.proximity.allocation`, and `xrspatial.proximity.direction` in `xrspatial.proximity` so that calls using `distance_metric='GREAT_CIRCLE'` with a finite `max_distance` produce the same observable results as equivalent eager numpy rasters. Pixels near one longitude edge must consider in-range targets near the opposite edge reachable across the +/-180 longitude seam, and high-latitude cases must use great-circle short-way longitude reachability so reachable targets are not treated as out of range.
