In `xrspatial.accessor`, ensure `XrsSpatialDataArrayAccessor.open_geotiff` and `XrsSpatialDatasetAccessor.open_geotiff` warn users only when called with `coregister=True` and the source GeoTIFF footprint covers more than 0% but less than 10% of the caller grid. The emitted `UserWarning` must state that the source covers only a percentage of the caller grid, that the coregistered result will be mostly NaN, and that users should open the small raster first and crop the caller before coregistering. Do not emit this sparse-coverage warning when coverage is at least 10%, and do not apply it to `auto_reproject=True`. A warning-triggering `coregister=True` call must still return a raster aligned to the caller grid, and the same warning behavior must apply when the caller grid and GeoTIFF use different coordinate reference systems.
